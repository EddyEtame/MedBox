## The one design decision, and the two ways to break it

**The AI never touches a measurement.** Sensors → NEWS2 → screen is the fast
track and has no model in it anywhere. Hypotheses, narration, the microphone
and now the phrase book are the slow track, and the slow track is allowed to
die.

**In Python.** Nothing in `server/triage.py`, `server/quarantine.py`,
`server/bus.py`, `server/db.py` or `server/sensors/` may import from
`server/ai/`, in any form. `tests/test_the_measurement_path_never_imports_the_ai.py`
resolves every import and fails on `from server.ai import …`, `import
server.ai.x`, `from .. import ai`.

**In the browser, which is where it actually broke.** A missing file is not an
import error, it is `undefined`, and `undefined.speakText(...)` thrown inside
the WebSocket handler freezes the board on plausible numbers. Every call into
`MedBox.voice` or `MedBox.mic` is guarded **on the same line**
(`if (MedBox.voice) MedBox.voice.speakText(x)`); the guard test reads call
sites line by line. Both views also carry a repaint watchdog: two seconds
without a completed repaint turns the link chip red and dims the numbers.

Also true, and just as load-bearing: **the operator enters deltas, never
values**; **isolation is proposed by the station and decided by a person**;
**a timeout is slow, not dead** (the assistant's `slow` flag is shown, never
announced; only the probe may say it has gone); **a reading NEWS2 scored
zero is not a sign**, whatever the model says about it.

## What is built (as of Wednesday evening)

- **NEWS2 triage** (`server/triage.py`): RCP 2017, all seven parameters:
  five from instruments (temperature, SpO₂, pulse, respiration, systolic
  pressure from a simulated cuff) and two a person enters from the panel
  (ACVPU consciousness, supplemental oxygen, `/api/patient/{id}/observations`).
  Absent ones are assumed normal and the score says it is a partial screen;
  entered, the label reads « NEWS2 complet, sept paramètres ». Single-
  parameter-3 rule. French reasons. Deterministic. Works with the AI off.
- **Personal healthy baselines** (`server/db.py`, `server/sensors/synthetic.py`):
  forty crew members, each with a baseline inside the NEWS2 zero bands,
  persisted with provenance. Scenarios and the console apply changes to that
  baseline; readings are append-only with their source; replay is a function
  of elapsed simulation time, not the wall clock.
- **Isolation in three states** (`server/quarantine.py`): candidate (scenario
  exposure + fever with desaturation or raised respiration), confirmed by a
  person (berth allocated), released only by a person. The zone rail, the
  crew panel and the announcer all speak this.
- **The 3D ship** (`web/ship.js`, raw WebGL) and **the flat board**
  (`web/app.js`): same feed, French, scenario picker with French names and
  the demo first, timeline with a real end state, trend of the last ninety
  observations, controlled simulation, local medical reports, protocol cards.
- **The assistant** (`server/ai/`): Ollama, `qwen2.5:1.5b-instruct`, French
  system prompt with a one-shot example, schema-constrained; the validator
  blocks drugs, doses and routes in both languages, refuses "nothing to go
  on" under a raised band, drops any sign that cites a reading NEWS2 scored
  zero, and **names every hypothesis from the instruments** (« Fièvre avec
  désaturation ») when the model's name is a disease or a sentence.
  `enforce()` rebuilds the answer from the schema's keys.
- **The answer is already there** (`MedBox.prefetch_ai`): the station assesses
  the worst crew members on its own as soon as they leave routine, one at a
  time, with a 90 s ceiling; a click returns the held answer in about a
  second, marked `cached` with its age, when it was written against the NEWS2
  total on screen. `?fresh=1` asks again. The operator's ceiling is 40 s.
- **MedBox learns from every request** (`server/learning.py`,
  `server/ai/intent.py`): a phrase the allow-list does not know goes to the
  phrase book, then to the model, which may only choose among the station's
  own actions under an enum grammar. What it decides once is written down and
  deterministic after; an operator's correction (`/api/assistant/feedback`)
  outranks it; every request is journaled; the Help panel shows what was
  learned. It cannot add an action, and an explicit « déclare : … » is never
  reinterpreted.
- **Listening** (`web/mic.js`, `web/voice.js`): one click for the microphone,
  a spoken French consent notice (`web/speech/consent_fr.wav`), consent by
  voice only (« j'accepte », « oui », "I accept", "yes"), then an energy VAD
  cutting the stream into utterances transcribed locally by faster-whisper
  (language detected per utterance). Only an utterance with the wake word
  « MedBox » is acted on. No browser speech recognition, ever: that is Google.
- **The station's voice**: 59 clips rendered with Piper `fr_FR-siwis-medium`
  (CC BY 4.0) and committed, so the demo laptop needs no speech engine and no
  French Windows voice (this laptop has none). Lines are French, twelve
  words at most, and every isolation state is announced.
- **Protocol cards** (`server/protocols.py`, `protocols/`): six French cards
  with official sources, fail-closed gates, a simulated inventory, no doses.
  The model never sees a card.
- **A portable folder** (`launcher/`, `tools/build-portable.ps1`,
  `packaging/`): a .NET 8 launcher that picks free ports and starts the
  bundled Ollama and the station as children it owns. **Never yet built or
  launched cold**: that is Thursday's first job.
- **The kill moment keeps its answer**: with the assistant dead, a click
  returns what it wrote before, dated and labelled « Réponse conservée ».
  « Rejouer » resets and restarts the scenario identically.
- **`tools\preflight.ps1`**: the Friday-morning check, one screen, changes
  nothing. **`LICENSE`**: Apache-2.0.
- **337 tests**: `.\.venv\Scripts\python -m pytest tests\ -q`.

## Measured on the demo laptop (i7-1255U, CPU only, station running)

| | |
|---|---|
| French prompt | 493 tokens; 14.6 s to evaluate when its prefix is not cached, 0.1 s when it is |
| generation | 14–15.5 tokens/s; one assessment 237–290 tokens |
| one assessment, warm | 18–36 s; the click ceiling is 40 s, the prefetch's 90 s |
| a click on a prefetched crew member | 1–2 s |
| warm-up, real prompt | 2.6 s; it caches the prefix the first assessment needs |
| introduction | 3.1 s; same system prompt as an assessment, so it evicts nothing |
| intent classification | one enum token; 8 s ceiling |
| board during an assessment | 9–10 frames a second |

The cache rule: any request under a *different* system prompt evicts the
prefix and the next assessment costs 38 s. Every call in `server/ai/` uses
`SYSTEM_PROMPT` for that reason. Keep it that way.

## Traps already paid for

**Windows PowerShell 5.1**: native stderr becomes an error record; under
`Stop` it is terminating (setup.ps1 uses `Continue` and `2>&1`). A relative
path needs `.\`. `Get-Command` proves a file is on PATH and nothing more;
probe the version. `Set-Location -LiteralPath`. Clear `$LASTEXITCODE` before
the call you check. Keep `.ps1` files ASCII. A quoted path followed by
arguments is an expression: use `& "path" args`. `Start-Process -Wait` on an
installer that launches a tray app never returns.

**Python and the console**: `cp1252` has no `✓`. Detect a venv with
`sys.prefix`. `input()` only prompts when stdin *and* stdout are ttys. Stale
`.pyc`: delete `__pycache__` before believing a surprising test. `bool("false")`
is True. Every `httpx.AsyncClient()` builds an SSL context (0.6–1.7 s on the
event loop): pass the one built at import. A user turn in English under a
French system prompt gets an English answer: the last thing the model read
wins.

**Ollama on Windows**: killing `ollama.exe` alone does nothing lasting; the
tray app restarts it in five seconds. `tools\assistant.ps1 stop|start`. A
different system prompt evicts the prefix cache. `num_thread = 6` on this
CPU (2P+8E); 12 threads run at 3 tokens/s.

**The browser**: `[hidden]` loses to any `display:` rule (both stylesheets
now carry `[hidden]{display:none !important}`). Rows rebuilt at 10 Hz
swallow clicks (select on `pointerdown`) and drop focus (restore it). A late
assessment lands under whoever is selected unless the id is pinned. A lost
WebGL context is a black void unless `webglcontextlost` is handled. Open the
page: two defects a day are only visible there.

**Verification**: a guard test is only worth having if you have watched it go
red. Transcribe rendered clips back with the station's own speech model:
that is how « Zone A » and « sept ou plus » were found wrong out loud.
